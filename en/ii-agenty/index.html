FAQ

Will the AI make things up?

We configure it to answer only from your materials and to say so honestly when it doesn’t know.

Where does the AI get its answers?

From your knowledge base: price lists, terms, policies, product descriptions.

Can it work in Telegram?

Yes — on your website, in Telegram and in other channels at the same time.

Shall we discuss your task?

Describe it in a couple of sentences — we’ll suggest how to build it and give a rough timeline.
