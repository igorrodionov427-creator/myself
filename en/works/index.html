Online store · notifications

Website orders — straight to Telegram

For a sports nutrition store we built notifications: every new order and support message instantly reaches the owner in Telegram.

  • Order contents. Items, quantity, promo code and total.
  • Delivery and contacts. Customer’s country, city, address, phone and email.
  • Payments. Payment method and transaction details — to quickly match the payment.
  • Support messages. Customer questions from the website arrive in the same chat, with the language noted.
Custom software · example

An SMM panel for managing clients’ social media

For the Apolline agency we built their own panel instead of subscription services. Add a client, connect their social accounts, upload a month’s content plan — and posts go out on schedule, even with your computer off.

  • Clients and notes. A list of clients, each with their own color, agreements and preferences.
  • Scheduled publishing. YouTube Shorts, Instagram, X, Facebook, Threads, Telegram channel, VK. For TikTok — a reminder with ready text and file.
  • Batch posting. 30 videos, “every day at 6 pm”, captions — and a whole month is scheduled with one button.
  • Calendar and queue. See what’s published, what’s queued and where an error occurred; the server retries on failure.
  • Data stays with the owner. Runs on your own server, clients’ keys aren’t stored with third-party services, no limit on the number of clients.
