Custom software · Windows apps

Small apps for a specific task

Sometimes you need neither a website nor a subscription service — just a simple app that does one thing well. We built two of them for ourselves and use them every day.

Calls and outreach tracker

  • The whole base in one table. Company, website, phones, email, who to call, the director, region and industry.
  • Import exports. Load a CSV table — new companies are added, known ones are filled in, and your notes are never overwritten.
  • “Called” in one click. Date, call result (no answer, call back, asked for an email, interested, client) and a note. The row is coloured by result.
  • See who was already emailed. A sent-email mark and filters: called, not called, call back, called but no email yet.
  • Search everything. By name, website, email and even phone digits. Click a website to open it in the browser.
  • Data is never lost. Everything saves automatically after each change, with a backup copy and Excel export.

Video voice-over in a minute

  • Three steps. Pick a video, paste the text, press “Voice the video” — a finished file with the voice-over appears next to it.
  • AI voice. Several ready voices or your own voice by ID, in Russian and other languages.
  • Settings per video. Speech speed, original audio volume, delay before the voice-over starts.
  • Never cuts the text. If the voice-over is longer than the video, the last frame is extended.
  • Social-media-ready sound. Loudness is normalized to platform standards; voice and background are mixed automatically.
  • Preview first. A voice preview button and an estimate of how many seconds the text will take.
Outreach · example

Email and WhatsApp outreach

Two client-acquisition tools we built for a video production company: an email outreach bot and a WhatsApp messaging app. Both find contacts on their own and send carefully so the mailbox and number don’t get blocked.

Email outreach bot

  • Finds emails on its own. Upload a company list to Telegram — the bot visits their websites, finds addresses and picks the right one (pr@, marketing@, info@). Irrelevant companies are filtered out by industry code.
  • Email drafts. Several ready templates; the company name is inserted into the subject and body.
  • Multiple mailboxes. Connect any mailbox in three steps; the bot detects the server itself. A copy of every email lands in “Sent”.
  • Warm-up for new mailboxes. Starts with a few emails a day and gradually increases the volume.
  • Protection from blocking. Daily and hourly limits, pauses between emails, sending only during business hours on weekdays. After bounces the mailbox pauses itself.
  • No repeats. Never writes to anyone twice and removes non-existent addresses from the queue.
  • Runs on a server 24/7. The queue continues on its own; statistics for any period and per mailbox.

WhatsApp outreach app

  • Numbers from anywhere. Paste a list in any format, load TXT, CSV or Excel — or collect mobile numbers from company websites in a new export.
  • Checks WhatsApp. Before sending it checks whether the number has WhatsApp and marks those without it.
  • One number per company. Never messages anyone twice and keeps a log of everything.
  • Safe pace. Pauses between messages, limits per run and per day, business hours and weekdays.
  • Easy to control. Its own window: message text, test send, WhatsApp account switch, progress log.
  • Statistics in Excel. Who was messaged, who has no WhatsApp, how many were sent per day.
