FAQ

How long does it take to build a bot?

A simple lead bot is quick; bots with bookings, payments and integrations take longer. We give an estimate after discussing the task.

Where does the bot run?

On a server, around the clock. We set up hosting and monitor that the bot stays online.

Can I see how your bot works?

Yes — message our bot @voriq_applicationsBot. It’s built the same way as our client bots.

Do you build bots for MAX?

Yes. One scenario can run in both Telegram and MAX with a shared request database.

Can the bot send requests to a CRM?

Yes: amoCRM, Bitrix24, Google Sheets and other systems.

Shall we discuss your task?

Describe it in a couple of sentences — we’ll suggest how to build it and give a rough timeline.
