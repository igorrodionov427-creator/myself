FAQ.

Didn’t find an answer? Message us — we reply within a day.

Where do we start?

Message us on Telegram, write to the bot @voriq_applicationsBot or leave a request on the site. A couple of words about the task is enough — we’ll ask the follow-up questions.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the task and, if you have them, examples you like. Texts, photos and access can come later — we’ll tell you exactly what’s needed.

Do you work under a contract?

Yes. Scope, timeline and stages are fixed in writing before we start, so both sides know what will be ready and when.

Who owns the code and access?

You do. We hand over the source code, domain, hosting, admin panels and bots in your name — no lock-in.

Can we start with a small task?

Yes. Many clients start with one bot or a landing page and add automation later. It’s an easy way to see how we work.

Can you improve my existing website or bot?

Yes. We’ll look at what you have and tell you what’s worth improving and what’s easier to rebuild.

How do work and communication go?

One Telegram chat for everything. We show interim versions and make edits along the way — no long calls or approvals.

Do you set up payments, CRM and analytics?

Yes: payments, CRM, Google Sheets, broadcasts, Telegram notifications and analytics — all connected into one system.

Do you build websites and bots in English?

Yes, in English and Russian. We work remotely with clients from different cities and countries.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We handle hosting, the domain, integrations and launch.

What happens after launch?

We stay in touch: edits, support, updates and improvements as your business grows.
