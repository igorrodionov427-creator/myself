AI agent vs. chatbot — what's the difference for a business

What an AI agent actually is, which tasks it can own, and when a simple button-based bot is the better choice.

A classic chatbot follows a script: button, answer, next step. An AI agent understands free text, relies on your own documents and can take actions — look things up, fill in a CRM record, create a task.

How they differ

Scripted chatbotAI agent
Customer questionsOnly what's in the menuAnything, in their own words
KnowledgeWhatever is written into the scriptYour knowledge base, price lists, policies
ActionsSend a leadFind, compare, fill in, hand over to a human

Where an agent pays off

  • First-line support. Answers routine questions 24/7 and escalates tricky cases.
  • Inbound triage. Reads each request, works out what the customer needs and routes it.
  • Working with documents. Finds answers in policies, contracts and manuals so staff don't have to.
  • Lead qualification. Clarifies the task and timeline before a salesperson steps in.

When you don't need AI

If there are only a few questions and they're always the same, a button-based bot is cheaper and more predictable. AI earns its keep where there are too many variations to script by hand.

How to roll it out without regrets

  1. Start with one job, such as answering FAQs.
  2. Build a knowledge base from what your team answers today.
  3. Always keep a path to a real person.
  4. Review real conversations and keep improving answers.
