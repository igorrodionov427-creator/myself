A Telegram bot for your business — what it does and who needs one

The jobs a Telegram bot takes off your team, why it beats a website form, and where to start if you don't have one yet.

More and more customers message a business instead of emailing or filling in a form. A Telegram bot lets you take those requests at any hour, ask the right questions and hand you a ready-to-use lead — without someone glued to the phone.

What a bot can do

  • Collect leads. It asks 4–6 questions, checks the phone or email and sends you a clean lead card.
  • Book appointments. Service, date and time — plus a reminder the day before.
  • Take payments right in the chat.
  • Answer common questions — hours, address, timelines, terms.
  • Send broadcasts to people who have already messaged the bot.
  • Work with your CRM or spreadsheets — leads land where you already track them.

Why a bot beats a form

A form gets filled in and the visitor is gone. In a bot the conversation continues: you can clarify details, they can send a voice note or come back later. And you keep a Telegram contact that's easy to reply to.

Who benefits first

  • Booking-based services: salons, clinics, workshops, studios.
  • Anyone getting leads from ads and social media and losing them at night and on weekends.
  • Shops that need a simple catalog and ordering in a messenger.
  • Teams that copy requests from chats into a spreadsheet by hand.

Where to start

Start with a lead bot: it's the fastest to launch and immediately shows how many requests you used to miss. Payments, bookings and broadcasts can come next.
