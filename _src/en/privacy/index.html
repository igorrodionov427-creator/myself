2. What data we receive

Only what you provide in a request on the website or in the bot:

To reply to your request, discuss the task and prepare a work plan. We don't use your data for marketing emails and we don't sell it.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only exception is technical delivery: requests from the website and the bot reach us through the Telegram messenger, and bot data is stored on our own server. We may disclose data otherwise only when lawfully required by public authorities.

5. How long we keep it

We keep requests while we're in contact and working with you, and no longer than 1 year after the last contact. We'll delete them sooner on request.

6. Cookies and browser data

  • The website uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • Browser storage (localStorage) only keeps a note that you closed the cookie notice, so it isn't shown again.
  • Fonts, icons and styles load from external services (Fontshare, Iconify, Tailwind CDN), and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, these services see technical request data such as your IP address and browser type.

7. Your rights

At any time you can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent. Message us on Telegram @rydr3 — we'll handle the request within 10 business days.

By sending a request on the website or in the bot, you agree to this policy and consent to the processing of the data above for the purposes described.
