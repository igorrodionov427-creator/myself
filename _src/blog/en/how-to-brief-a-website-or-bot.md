You don't need a long technical spec. But the clearer the task, the more accurate the plan and timeline — and the fewer rewrites. Here's what to prepare; a few lines per point is plenty.

## 1. Your business and customers

What you do, who you sell to and what sets you apart. One paragraph is enough.

## 2. The problem the project should solve

Not "we need a website", but "we need leads from ads" or "we're tired of copying orders from chats by hand". The goal defines the solution.

## 3. What the customer should do

Call, leave a request, book, pay, ask a question. Ideally one main action.

## 4. What you already have

- Website, bot, social media, CRM, spreadsheets.
- Logo and brand colors.
- Copy, photos, case studies, testimonials.
- Domain and hosting.

## 5. Examples you like

Two or three websites or bots — yours or anyone's — and what you like about them. It saves hours of back-and-forth.

## 6. What it should connect to

Payments, CRM, Google Sheets, broadcasts, Telegram notifications — anything that should work together with the project.

## 7. Deadline and decision-maker

Is there a launch date, and who signs off on your side?
